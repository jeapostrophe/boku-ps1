# NOTES
# * E0406.5 is the game telling the player how to open the forest: the beehive really is knocked
#   down with the fishing rod (E0205). Boku's solemn promise in .6 is the joke. Keep both literal.
# * "When Uncle was a little brat, he..." — adults here refer to themselves by their role when
#   talking to Boku. English does this with small children too, so it is kept (style-guide
#   § Self-reference). The uncle switches to plain "I" (boku) when he talks to his wife — that
#   contrast is worth keeping audible in the English.
# * E0406.8 is the set reply to gochisosama (literally "it was a poor meal"). Recommended:
#   translated, because unlike itadakimasu it is not something the player hears ten times a day
#   and it has a transparent meaning. The alternative is "Osomatsusama deshita."
