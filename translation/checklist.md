# Checklist (PLAN `TRN-08`)

The settled renderings the re-translation of days 1–7 got wrong most often, although every one
is already in the style guide or the glossary. The packet puts this list at the end of each
translator's `system.md`. It adds no rule: each line cites its home, and when this list and its
home disagree, the home is right and this list is the one to fix. Licence: CC BY-SA 4.0
(`LICENSE-translation`).

* **Interjections are the style guide § 7's**: *un* → "Yeah" / "Mm-hm", never "Un"; *are?* →
  "Huh?" / "Wait..."; *ē?* → "Huh?"; *hē* → "Ohh". Glossary § 8 lists the rest.
* **Laughter is spelled as § 7 spells it**: "hehe", "ehehe", "ahaha" as they are; *fufu* →
  "Hm-hm"; *gehehe* → "Geh-heh-heh".
* **Capitals only for a word someone shouts, and sparingly** (§ 9). Not for emphasis, not for a
  whole line.
* **Nothing added** (`translation/days/README.md` § Lessons): no intensifier ("really",
  "so", "just"), no connective ("and then", "so", "but") and no explanation the Japanese does
  not have.
* **Nothing dropped**: the small words carry the meaning. *mo* ("too", "also", "even") and
  *dake* ("only", "just") are translated, not left out (§ 0: nothing is cut).
* **The glossary's renderings are settled**: a name, a place, an insect, a dish or a set phrase
  the glossary lists is written its way, every time, including its capitals.
* **No em dash** unless the Japanese breaks the line with one (§ 18).
* **Notes are English**: no Japanese in a `# NOTE` or `# UNSURE` line; romanise a word you
  quote (`translation/days/README.md` § Format).
