# Glossary (PLAN `TRN-01`)

Every recurring term that must come out one way. **English** is what [style-guide.md](style-guide.md)
produces, in force since Jay's rulings of 2026-09-20; **rejected** (and "alt." inside a note)
is the alternative that was put to him and not chosen, kept so nobody re-proposes it without a
new reason. A `Qn` in a note points at the question as recorded in
[QUESTIONS.md](QUESTIONS.md). "Where" gives line ids or array names only — the Japanese for
them is in a local import. Readings are in Hepburn with macrons; *game text drops the macrons*
(style guide § 3, Q9). Species identifications are from general knowledge of the Japanese
fauna and are marked † where they should be checked against a field guide before they are
printed in the insect book.

> **Before this file is first committed — Jay's call.** A glossary needs its source terms, so
> §§ 4a and 5 list the Japanese for all 57 insect names and the 48 dinner dishes. They are
> species names and dish names — common nouns, not the game's writing — but between them they
> do reproduce the contents of `exe@8003D2E0` and the option rows of `E0221`, and CLAUDE.md's
> rule is "a short quoted line is fine; a table of them is not". If that rule is read to cover
> word lists, the fix is mechanical: drop the source column from those two tables and key the
> rows by array index / quiz day, which are already there.

Source tags: [script] read in the decoded text; [walkthrough] jooey's FAQ; [review] Action
Button; [web] see `reference/SOURCES.md`; [inference].

## 4. Play: insects, sumo, fishing, kites

Class words are always English: beetle, stag beetle, rhinoceros beetle, butterfly, swallowtail,
dragonfly, cicada, cricket, firefly, ant (*arinko* → "ants", "little ants").

### 4a. The 57 insect names (`exe@8003D2E0.*`, entries `hhon@5328.*`) — Q6, ruled 2026-09-20

The rule, per class (Jay: *"we want a mix"*): **butterflies, beetles, dragonflies → English;
cicadas and the singing insects → the Japanese name**, because those are named after their
calls, the entries spell the calls out, and the game plays them. In the insect book the entry's first line is the name;
where the entry jokes about the *Japanese* name (marked ✎) that line carries both:
"Chinese Peacock (Karasu-ageha, 'crow swallowtail')".
