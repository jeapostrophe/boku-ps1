# --- E0210: the workshop (H26), before 16:00, talking to the uncle. .0-.6 the first time; .7 on

E0210.4	Uncle	Hmmm, actually, you know, firing the kiln in summer is hot, so honestly I'd rather not... // but by late August there'll probably be quite a pile of things to fire... // so around then, I suppose I'll have to light it.

E0210.6	Uncle	Uncle's looking forward to it too — apart from the heat.
E0210.7	Uncle	Oh — still here, are you. // At Uncle's house there's a back gate next to the veranda... // and from there, by way of the bridge, you can get out toward the mountains. // A child should go and play out in the great outdoors. // The mountains at night are dangerous, so the gate's only open in the daytime, though.
# NOTE E0210.1, page 3: the studio name is explained from the place name, so both stay
#   "Tsukiyono" (glossary § 2). Page 4: "the wheel" is the potter's wheel (rokuro).
# NOTE E0210.2: "climbing kiln" — the uncle names it from its shape on day 25 (glossary § 2).
# NOTE E0210.4: no self-reference in the Japanese; "I" throughout, "Uncle" only in .6 where
#   he says it.
# NOTE E0210.7, page 4: いらっしゃい is the uncle sending him off — "go and play" — with no
#   "should" in the Japanese beyond the imperative; the English needs a verb of permission or
