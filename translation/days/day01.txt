# NOTES — the day
# * Register. The uncle: warm, teacherly, "Uncle" of himself with Boku (E0114.5, E0113.5), plain
#   "me" to his wife (E0113.6); mock-official when he lays down rules or appointments (E0114.7,
#   E0190.0). The aunt: quick and teasing, "Auntie" of herself (E0121.0, E0181.0, E0113.3), "you
#   know" / "all right?" for her feminine sentence endings. Boku: short, polite, literal; "Yeah"
#   for "un"; he calls his parents Mom and Dad. Shirabe: curt and prim. The narrator: full
#   sentences, no contractions, past tense, "my uncle" / "my father".
# * Pages. Every voiced line keeps the disc's page count and order; the "//" breaks are the
#   original's. No page runs past the dialogue band (4 lines of ~46) — no OVERFLOW on day 1.
# * Duplicates. E0173.0 = E0174.0, E0177.1 = E0179.0, E0177.2 = E0179.1 (identical source text,
#   identical English — bible § 8 item 4).
