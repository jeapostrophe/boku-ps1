# --- E1701: G02 -- breakfast (the Bath Lady is back)
E1701.0	Shirabe	They say the Bath Lady came to our house this morning. // She came late this year, didn't she?
E1701.1	Aunt	That's right. Usually she comes bringing souvenirs from the Sendai Tanabata festival.
E1701.2	Moe	I wonder where she's pitched her tent? // Last year it was around the Great Ginkgo.
E1701.3	Aunt	This year it looks like she's going in as far as Dragon God Pond.
E1701.4	Boku	Question.
E1701.5	Aunt	Yes?
E1701.6	Boku	Who is she?
E1701.7	Uncle	Wolf... Girl. // She's a pretty university student who shuts herself away in these mountains every summer, // and she comes to our house to borrow the bath.
E1701.8	Aunt	She brings loads of books and lives in a tent. // If you go left at the Jizo-sama and through the forest, there's a big pond, isn't there?
E1701.9	Uncle	This year she seems to have pitched her tent around there, // so you should go and have a look too, Boku-kun.
E1701.10	Uncle	Well then, itadakimaasu.
E1701.11	Shirabe, Moe, Boku, Aunt	Itadakimaasu.
# NOTE E1701.7: the nickname spelled out syllable by syllable in the Japanese; the English words
#   said slowly (style guide § 7).
