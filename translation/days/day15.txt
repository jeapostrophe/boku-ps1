E1503.7	Uncle	Hahaha, you know some hard words.
E1503.8	Uncle	Then thank you for all your hard work.
E1503.9	Uncle	Well then, itadakimasu!
E1503.10	Shirabe, Moe, Boku, Aunt	Itadakimasu!
# NOTE E1503.5: the first two are four-character idioms; the second, shuchi-nikurin ("a lake of
#   wine and a forest of meat"), means an orgy of feasting, hence the uncle's "hard words"
#   (bible § 6). Translated literally.
