# --- E2514: H15 -- the climbing kiln (the firing begins)
# VOICE E2514.0: just -- carries chanto with yakusoku-doori: he came properly, just as promised
E2514.0	Uncle	Boku-kun, you came, just like you promised. // Now, I'll light the fire in the kiln's firebox.
E2514.1	Uncle	This kiln... // is built, as you can see, as if it's climbing the slope, so it's called a climbing kiln. // With the kiln this shape, when you burn a fire at the bottom, the heat rises by itself toward the top of the slope, // so you can bring the temperature inside up past a thousand degrees efficiently. // That's why it's a hooot job, and not one I'm too keen on in midsummer.
E2514.2	Boku	What happens after this?
E2514.3	Uncle	First, for a while, I feed in lots of firewood every thirty or forty minutes. // Then, from around dawn, I gradually speed that up, and...
E2514.4	Boku	So you're up all night.
E2514.5	Uncle	Ahaha, well, of course. // And after that, around early tomorrow afternoon, when the inside reaches the target of 1,200 degrees, // I work hard to hold it there for about two hours...
E2514.6	Boku	Uh-huh, uh-huh.
E2514.7	Uncle	and around evening I seal up this firebox and put the fire out.
E2514.8	Boku	And then it's done?
E2514.9	Uncle	Yeah! // But I have to wait for it to cool down on its own before opening the kiln, // so you'll have to wait till the evening of the day after tomorrow to meet what's been fired.
E2514.10	Boku	Whew... that's a long wait.
E2514.11	Uncle	I'm getting hungry already.
