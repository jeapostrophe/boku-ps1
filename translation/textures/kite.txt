# --- the three labels along the bottom of the screen while a kite is up. direction (風向,
# wind direction) stands beside a compass whose needle is the wind; speed (風速, wind speed) is
# over a number in m/h; altitude (高度) is over the kite's height in m. direction and speed are
# neighbours on one row, so the row reads "Wind Speed" over the number with the compass
# beside it, and the compass draws the direction. The three are instrument words in the
# Japanese (高度, not 高さ), so altitude, not height. The unit m/h is the game's own drawing and
# stays. A label is one line in the game's glyphs; one too wide for its cell is refused, and
# the refusal says how much room there is.
kite@hud.direction	Wind
kite@hud.speed	Speed
kite@hud.altitude	Altitude
