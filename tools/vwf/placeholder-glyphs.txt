# Punctuation for the game's own Latin set -- the cells the font sheet lacks in a
# horizontal form, or has in a form that sits wrong beside the Latin letters
# (research/font.md § "What the font offers English"; PLAN TXT-08).
#
# EVERY CELL HERE IS DRAWN FOR THIS PROJECT (Jay's TXT-08 ruling, 2026-09-20), to the
# sheet's own weight: one-pixel strokes, and dots like the sheet's own `．` and `：` (2 x 2;
# its `？` and `！` end in a 1 x 2 dot), on the Latin letters' rows -- cap height rows 1-9,
# baseline row 9, descenders to row 10 -- and checked in the game beside them. Where there
# is only one way to draw a mark (a 2 x 2 dot, a mirrored paren) the drawing may coincide
# with the sheet's; nothing was copied out of it. The harvest alternative was measured and
# not taken: Ark Pixel 12 and Galmuri set `.` and `:` as 1 x 1 dots and `'` as a 1 x 3
# tick, lighter than the sheet (research/vwf-prototype.md § "Round 3"). No harvested cell,
# so no licence to record.
#
# Why `.` `,` `:` `;` are here although the sheet has horizontal forms of them
# (glyph-table.tsv; the vertical `、` `。` are cells 1-2): the sheet's dot sits on rows 7-8,
# a row ABOVE the Latin baseline, which is the floating full stop Jay saw; these sit on it.
#
# Format (tools/vwf/build_prototype.py, `load_glyph_file`): `glyph U+XXXX advance N`, then
# twelve rows of twelve cells, `#` ink and `.` paper, column 0 at the left; a comment
# line is `#` alone or `# ...`. A glyph may carry a left bearing (paper before the ink);
# the advance must reach past the ink and its shadow. The same format, covering a whole
# typeface, is what `--font` takes.
